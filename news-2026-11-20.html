Big Homie Cash spotlight: "Back and forth" featuring Freezzo

Today's DMF Records spotlight goes to the label's founder, Columbus, Ohio rapper Big Homie Cash. "Back and forth" came out June 18, 2023 on DMF Records Fly Hoolie Ent, a 3:43 hip-hop cut where he trades verses with labelmate Freezzo.

Big Homie Cash started DMF Records in Columbus in 2009. His biggest record on Spotify so far is "Running man," with more than 56,000 plays. Run "Back and forth" back, then go through the rest of his catalog on his artist page.

Freezzo spotlight: "Hotter Then Hell" featuring Chef Lo

Freezzo is a west side Columbus, Ohio rapper on DMF Records. "Hotter Then Hell," featuring Chef Lo, dropped January 13, 2020 and is still his top song on YouTube Music with about 3,600 plays.

Freezzo has more DMF releases than anyone on the roster, from "No fake shit" to "Calling my cellular" and "Emotions." Start with "Hotter Then Hell" and keep going.

OBMB Delo spotlight: "13 reasons"
