DMF Promo

Real promotion for independent artists, run by DMF Records in Columbus, Ohio

Pick a package, send a request, and DMF Records reviews it and confirms the details with you before anything is charged. A request is not a payment: nothing is billed on this page.

DMF Promo uses real ads and reaches real listeners only. We never sell subscribers, followers, watch hours, or streams, and we never use bots. DMF is not a Spotify editorial partner, and no playlist placement is guaranteed. Results vary and no numbers are guaranteed. Ad spend is paid to the ad platform and shown separately from DMF's service fee.
