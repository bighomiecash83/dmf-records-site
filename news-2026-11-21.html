Columbus, Ohio hip-hop: DMF Records daily spotlight

November 21, 2026

Big Homie Cash spotlight: "Feeling my vibe" featuring Go Savage

Today's DMF Records spotlight is "Feeling my vibe" from Columbus, Ohio rapper Big Homie Cash. The single dropped February 23, 2024 and runs 2:44, with labelmate Go Savage on the feature.

It's one of his biggest records on Spotify, with more than 21,000 plays, second only to "Running man." If you like this one, the label's YouTube channel, DMF Music Platform, has over 3,100 subscribers and plenty more from the camp. Press play and turn it up.

Freezzo spotlight: "My Way"

West side Columbus, Ohio rapper Freezzo takes his time on "My Way." Released April 10, 2020, it runs 5 minutes and 52 seconds, one of the longest songs in the DMF Records catalog, and he carries it solo.

On Spotify, the song leading his popular list right now is "do it for the hood," his December 2020 collaboration with label founder Big Homie Cash. Run "My Way" first, then queue up the collab and hear how the two sound together.

Freezzo artist page · Listen to Freezzo on Spotify

OBMB Delo spotlight: "Know who you are"

OBMB Delo is a Columbus, Ohio rapper on the DMF Records roster. His single "Know who you are" came out April 19, 2024 and gets right to the point in 2 minutes and 22 seconds.

A month later he followed it with "Standing on my own 10," a six-track project released May 24, 2024. It includes "Lead by da train," "On my mind" and "Rather see me fall" featuring Big Homie Cash. Start with the single, then let the whole project ride.

OBMB Delo artist page · Listen to OBMB Delo on Spotify

Ellumf spotlight: "October 3"

Columbus, Ohio rapper Ellumf released "October 3" on January 14, 2024 through DMF Records Fly Hoolie Ent. It's 2 minutes and 22 seconds, and it's his most-played song on YouTube Music.

Ellumf also links up with labelmate Go Savage on "Pistol on da Dresser," released December 14, 2023. You can find his music on Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music, Deezer and Pandora. Pick your platform, play "October 3," and follow him there.

Ellumf artist page · Listen to Ellumf on Spotify

Go Savage spotlight: "Living right"

Go Savage represents Columbus, Ohio on the DMF Records roster. "Living right" is on his March 22, 2024 release "No hook," a 3:10 solo cut sitting next to the title track.

You've also heard him on two of Big Homie Cash's singles, "Running man" and "Feeling my vibe." "Running man" has passed 56,000 plays on Spotify. Stream "Living right" today and follow Go Savage for more.
