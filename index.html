DMF Records

Columbus, Ohio / Fly Hoolie Entertainment · Independent hip-hop since 2009

DMF Records is an independent hip-hop label from Columbus, Ohio, founded in 2009 by Deangelo "Big Homie Cash" Jackson and made official as an Ohio company in 2016. The label owns its masters and publishing, and has put out 61 tracks across 49 releases since 2020.

Built on ownership. Powered by innovation.

An independent music and technology company rooted in culture and driven by infrastructure. DMF controls its masters, builds its own systems, and develops AI-powered tools to run distribution, analytics, and artist growth, all in-house. No middlemen. No shortcuts. Just execution, legacy, and long-term equity.
